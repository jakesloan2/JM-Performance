In short

JM Performance is an ECU remapping specialist and VAG tuning workshop at 14 Blackstaff Road, Kircubbin, Newtownards BT22 1AQ, on the Ards Peninsula. We specialise in the Volkswagen Audi Group platforms — Volkswagen, Audi, SEAT and Škoda — and work on every other make of car, van and light commercial too. About

Built on files, not guesswork

JM Performance started the way most good workshops do — one person, a laptop and a reputation to protect. Every vehicle that comes through gets the same process: full health check, original file read and archived, custom calibration written for that engine, then road testing and data logging before it's handed back.

We tune with genuine, industry-standard hardware for full OBD and bench read/write access across nearly every modern petrol and diesel platform. That matters — cheap clone tools are how ECUs get bricked.

If a vehicle isn't a good candidate for tuning, we say so before you spend anything. A worn turbo, a slipping clutch or a stretched timing chain needs sorting first, and no map will hide it.

FAQ

Straight answers

What is an ECU remap?

An ECU remap is a rewrite of the software inside your engine control unit. We read your vehicle's original file, adjust the calibration tables that govern fuelling, boost pressure, ignition timing and torque limiters, then write the modified file back. Manufacturers set these conservatively to cover poor fuel, skipped servicing and extreme climates worldwide. A remap recovers that headroom for the conditions you actually drive in.

How much power will my car gain?

Turbocharged diesels typically gain 25–35% more power and a similar rise in torque. Turbocharged petrols usually gain 20–30%. Naturally aspirated engines gain far less — around 5–8% — because there is no boost to adjust, though throttle response improves noticeably. Enter your registration above for guide figures specific to your engine.

Is remapping safe for my engine?

Yes, when it's done properly on a healthy vehicle. We stay inside the manufacturer's safe limits for exhaust gas temperature, cylinder pressure and boost, and we log the car under load before handing it back. The risk comes from bad files written blind or from tuning a vehicle that already has a mechanical fault. That's why every job starts with a diagnostic health check.

Will a remap affect my warranty?

It can. A manufacturer may refuse a warranty claim on the engine, turbo, clutch or gearbox if they detect modified software. Most modern ECUs log a checksum or write count, so it is usually detectable even after a return to stock. If your vehicle is still under manufacturer warranty, weigh that up carefully — we're happy to talk it through honestly rather than tell you what you want to hear.

Do I need to tell my insurer about a remap?

Yes. A remap is a modification and must be declared to your insurer. Failing to declare it can invalidate your policy and leave a claim unpaid. In practice many insurers charge little or nothing for a Stage 1 map on a standard vehicle, but the declaration has to be made. We'll give you written details of the work for your insurer.

How long does a remap take?

Most Stage 1 and economy remaps take 60 to 90 minutes from arrival to road test. Stage 2 calibrations, gearbox tuning and anything requiring the ECU to be removed for bench work take two to three hours. If we're coming to you, you can carry on with your day while we work.

Will a remap improve my fuel economy?

It can, but only if your driving style stays the same. An economy map delivers torque lower in the rev range so you can change up earlier and hold a taller gear, which typically returns 8–15% better mpg on diesels doing steady motorway or commuting miles. If you use the extra power, you'll use more fuel — that's just physics.

Do you specialise in particular makes?

Yes. JM Performance is a VAG specialist, meaning Volkswagen, Audi, SEAT, Škoda and Cupra. Those four brands share engines, gearboxes and ECU hardware across the group, so we work daily on EA189 and EA288 diesels, EA211 and EA888 petrols and DQ250, DQ381 and DL382 gearboxes. Every other make is welcome and follows exactly the same process.

Where is the workshop and do I need to leave the car?

We are at 14 Blackstaff Road, Kircubbin, Newtownards BT22 1AQ, on the Ards Peninsula between Greyabbey and Portavogie. All work is carried out at the workshop — we are not a mobile service. Most jobs are done while you wait, so you can bring the car in and take it away the same visit. There is off-road parking on site.

Can my car be returned to standard?

Yes. Your original ECU file is read and archived before any changes are made, and we keep it indefinitely. If you're selling the vehicle, going in for warranty work, or simply don't get on with the map, we'll write the original file back free of charge.

Does a remap affect my MOT?

A properly written remap does not cause an MOT failure. Emissions must still fall within the limits for your vehicle, and a good map is calibrated to do exactly that. What does cause a failure is removing or disabling emissions equipment such as a DPF, EGR or SCR system — which we do not do on road-registered vehicles.

How much does a remap cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, the ECU type and whether the work can be done through the OBD port or needs bench access. Stage 1 and economy maps for common cars and vans sit at the lower end; Stage 2, gearbox tuning and bench jobs cost more.
